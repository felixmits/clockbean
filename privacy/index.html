Clockbean

Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Clockbean shows how much of your work shift is left, what share of it you've done, and how much money you've made so far. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What the app stores

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone only:

  • your usual shift: the hours for each day of the week, your hourly rate or monthly pay (optional), how breaks, overtime and night work are paid, which days of the week and which holidays pay more, and when to remind you to start;
  • the shift you're working: when you started it, when it's due to end, its breaks and tips;
  • your past shifts, and a savings goal if you set one;
  • your first name, if you give one, so Mungo, the app's mascot, can use it, and the kind of work you do, if you pick one, so Mungo can dress for it;
  • what Mungo wears, and what it has unlocked to wear (the party hat comes with the first savings goal you reach);
  • a few markers so it doesn't repeat itself, such as which milestone notifications it has already shown.

The app uses this only to show your shift on screen, on the home-screen widget and in its notifications.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and asks for no email or phone number; a first name only if you want Mungo to use it.
  • It does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your information anywhere.
  • It does not contain ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • It does not sell or share any information, because it doesn't collect any.
  • When you export your history, the app hands a file to the app you choose (email, cloud storage and so on). What happens to it then is up to you and that app.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup for your Google account, Android may include the app's settings in that backup so they come back on a new phone. That backup is made by Android and stored by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your shift on the lock screen and in the status bar, and to tell you when you're halfway, in your last hour, and when your shift is over, and to remind you when your shift starts.
  • Run while a shift is on (foreground service): so the time and money keep updating while you work. It starts only when you start a shift and stops when you end it.
  • Alarms & reminders: so the reminder to start your shift comes at the exact minute. You can take it away in your phone's settings; the reminder then comes up to 10 minutes early.
  • Start at boot: so a shift that was running carries on, and your reminder is set again, after your phone restarts.

Deleting your information

Your information is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.

Children

The app is meant for people who work shifts and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
